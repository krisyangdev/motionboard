# Light Leak Transition

A soft orange, amber and magenta glow drifts in from one side of the frame, peaks near white on the cut frame, and the next screen develops out of the overexposure. It happens twice, ending on the lockup.

## Why it works

A light leak is the orange, amber and magenta glow that stray light prints on film. This film uses it as a transition: light drifts in from one side, reaches near-white exactly on the cut frame, the scene is swapped on that brightest frame so no hard cut is visible, and then the light recedes while the new scene develops out of the overexposure. The glow is seven large radial gradients with different sizes, colors, paths and eases (the big orange one covers the middle with power3.out, the magenta one turns slowly, a narrow streak sweeps across with power1.inOut, and a deep red one stays on the bottom edge), so the shape reads as organic and not as one mask sliding across. The layer uses screen blending, which only adds light, so dark scenes pick up the warm color and it bleeds in naturally at the edges. Three exposure moves stack around the cut: the blobs, a near-white flash layer (peak opacity 0.98, with a 0.04 s plateau on each side of the cut; the three cuts sit on frames 84, 171 and 249, all inside the plateau) and an exposure lift on the scene itself (brightness 2.3, contrast 0.82, saturation 1.3), so the picture washes out from within. Grain rises from 0.5 to 0.85 during a leak, as it does on real overexposed film. All three scenes are real screens of Grain, a film-look camera app, so the theme and the effect agree: a light leak is a film defect and here it becomes the brand's language. The first leak is triggered by the shutter (pressed at 1.75 s, the glow enters at 1.8 s); the later ones land after each scene has finished its content, and the next scene's elements (gallery frames, the preview) develop out of the overexposure, which turns the transition and the next scene's entrance into one move. There is a single accent color, amber #ff9a2e; the orange and magenta only exist inside the light.

## Often confused with

- [Wipe and Flash Transition](./wipe-flash-transition.md): Foreground Wipe & Flash covers the frame with a large blurred object or a center white flash before the scene changes; the leak here is several organic blobs of different colors drifting from one side, with grain and an exposure lift, and the swap lands on the brightest frame.
- [Match Cut Transition](./match-cut-transition.md): Match Cut keeps the anchor position still and swaps one thing for another; a light leak needs no shared position between the two scenes, because the overexposure hides the cut and the scenes can be completely different.
