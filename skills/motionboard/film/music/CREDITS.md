# Music for the launch film

Three tracks by Kevin MacLeod (incompetech.com), each cut to its first 60 seconds and re-encoded at 160 kbps. Licensed under Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/): free for commercial use, credit required. Wherever a film with one of these tracks is posted, put its credit line in the description.

| File | Track | Tempo | Suits | Where it lifts |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `chill-wave.mp3` | Chill Wave | 100 BPM, atmospheric synth | dark brands, calm and premium products | steady from the start |
| `digital-lemonade.mp3` | Digital Lemonade | 120 BPM, bright electronic | light brands, consumer and playful products | fuller from about 18 s; `"start": 16` puts the lift near the first cut |
| `inspired.mp3` | Inspired | 120 BPM, uplifting electronica | tools for makers, creative and design products | steady from the start |
