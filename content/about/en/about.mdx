## What this site is

Site Template is a demonstration site. It shows every kind of page a site built from our template can have: tools that run in the browser, guides, hub pages, and the legal and about pages. It is written for people who build sites and for the teammates who fill them with content.

## Who runs it

The site is run by the Site Template team. To reach us, write to [hello@krisyang.dev](mailto:hello@krisyang.dev). We read every message.

## How pages are made and checked

Every page targets a search term that someone actually uses, and the page records where we checked that demand. Drafts are written with AI assistance and checked by hand against the real output of the tool or the steps they describe. Tools are tried in Chrome, Safari and Firefox before they are released.

Each page shows the date it was last updated. We change that date only when the content changes, not when we touch the layout. Guides list the sources of any facts about other products, with the date we checked them.

The build refuses to publish a page that is too short or has no first-hand material, so thin pages stay out of search results. You can read more in the [guides](/guides/) and the [tools](/tools/).

## Corrections

If you find a mistake, email [hello@krisyang.dev](mailto:hello@krisyang.dev) with the page address and what is wrong. We fix confirmed errors and update the date on the page.
