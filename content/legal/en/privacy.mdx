This policy explains what information this site handles. We keep it short because we collect very little.

## What the tools do with your data

Our tools run in your browser. The text, files and settings you enter are processed on your device and are not uploaded to our servers.

## Analytics

We measure page views and feature use with analytics that does not use cookies and does not follow you across other sites. We record events such as which page was opened and which button was used. We do not use this data to identify you personally or to build advertising profiles.

## Accounts

Creating an account is optional. If you do, we store the email address you sign up with and the settings tied to your account. We use them to sign you in and to run the service, and for nothing else.

## Payments

Paid plans are sold through a merchant of record, which handles checkout, taxes and invoices as the seller. Your card details go to the payment provider and are never seen or stored by us. We receive the information needed to confirm your purchase, such as your email address and plan.

## Sharing

We do not sell your data. We share it only with the service providers that run this site, such as hosting, analytics and payments, and only as far as they need to do their job, or when the law requires it.

## Your choices

You can ask us to see, correct or delete the data tied to your account. You can also stop using the site at any time without any data being kept about you beyond your account.

## Contact

For questions about your data, write to the email address in the footer.

## Changes

If this policy changes, we update this page and the date above.
